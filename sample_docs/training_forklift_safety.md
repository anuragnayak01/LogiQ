# Training Guide: Forklift Operation and Certification

## Who This Applies To
Any associate who operates a powered industrial truck (forklift, order picker,
pallet jack with rider platform) in a company facility.

## Certification Requirement
Operators must complete both classroom training and a hands-on evaluation
before operating any forklift unsupervised. Certification is valid for 3
years, and must be renewed earlier if the operator is involved in a
preventable incident or switches to a different equipment class.

## Pre-Shift Inspection Checklist
Before every shift, operators must complete the pre-shift checklist covering:
- Tire condition and pressure
- Forks: no visible cracks or bending
- Hydraulic fluid leaks
- Horn and backup alarm function
- Seatbelt condition
- Battery/fuel level

Any failed item must be reported to the Shift Supervisor immediately, and the
unit tagged "Out of Service" — it must not be operated until repaired.

## Operating Rules
- Maximum speed inside the warehouse is 5 mph, reduced to walking pace at
  intersections and blind corners.
- Loads must never exceed the rated capacity shown on the equipment data
  plate.
- Forks must be lowered fully and the parking brake engaged whenever the
  operator dismounts, even briefly.
- Pedestrians always have right of way; operators must make eye contact with
  pedestrians before proceeding.

## Incident Reporting
Any collision, near-miss, or dropped load must be reported to the Shift
Supervisor within 30 minutes, regardless of whether damage or injury
occurred. A near-miss report does not result in disciplinary action and is
used only for safety trend analysis.
