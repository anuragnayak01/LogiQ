# Policy: Leave and Attendance

## Purpose
Defines paid time off (PTO), sick leave, and attendance expectations for all
hourly and salaried employees.

## PTO Accrual
- Warehouse and driver staff (hourly): accrue 1 hour of PTO for every 30 hours
  worked, up to a maximum of 80 hours banked per calendar year.
- Salaried staff: accrue PTO at a flat rate of 15 days per year, front-loaded
  at the start of each calendar year.
- Unused PTO above the cap does not roll over and is forfeited on Jan 1,
  except where state law requires payout (check with HR for your state).

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO and accrues at 1 hour per 40 hours worked for
hourly employees, capped at 48 hours per year. No doctor's note is required
for absences of 2 days or fewer. Absences of 3+ consecutive days require a
return-to-work note from a medical provider.

## Attendance Points
- A no-call/no-show results in 4 attendance points.
- An unexcused late arrival (>10 minutes) results in 1 point.
- Points expire on a rolling 90-day basis.
- Accumulating 8 points in a rolling 90-day window triggers a documented
  coaching conversation with your supervisor. 12 points triggers a formal
  written warning per the Progressive Discipline Policy.

## Requesting Time Off
Requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 business days in
advance for planned absences. Emergency/same-day absences must be reported
by phone or text to your direct supervisor before your scheduled shift start.
