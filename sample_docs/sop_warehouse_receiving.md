# SOP: Warehouse Receiving Process

## Purpose
This procedure governs how inbound shipments are received, inspected, and logged
at all company distribution centers.

## Scope
Applies to all warehouse associates, dock supervisors, and inventory control staff.

## Procedure

1. **Dock Assignment**: Upon truck arrival, the dock supervisor assigns a bay
   number via the yard management system (YMS). Drivers must not be directed to
   a bay verbally without a YMS confirmation.

2. **Inspection**: Before unloading, the receiving associate performs a visual
   seal check. If the seal number does not match the bill of lading (BOL),
   escalate immediately to the Shift Supervisor and do NOT break the seal.

3. **Unloading & Counting**: Pallets are unloaded and counted against the
   packing list. Discrepancies of more than 2% of line-item quantity must be
   logged as a "Receiving Exception" in the WMS within the same shift.

4. **Damage Handling**: Any damaged units are set aside in the Damage Hold
   area (Zone D). Photograph all damage before disposal or return processing.
   Damage exceeding $500 estimated value requires Claims department sign-off
   before further action.

5. **Putaway**: Once counted and inspected, associates scan each pallet into
   its assigned storage location. Putaway must be completed within 4 hours of
   dock assignment for standard freight, or within 1 hour for temperature-
   controlled freight.

6. **Documentation**: The signed BOL, exception report (if any), and putaway
   confirmation are uploaded to the WMS document repository before the
   receiving record is closed.

## Exceptions
Hazmat shipments follow SOP-HZ-002 instead of this document. Cross-dock
shipments (no putaway) follow SOP-XD-004.
